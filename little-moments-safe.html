Where this Little started
The little story behind Little Moments ♥
this one started with the photos ✦

I was already taking the pictures. The hard part was keeping the little story that made each one worth remembering.

“I already take the pictures. Why is keeping them organized the hard part?”

A camera roll can hold the photo, but it doesn’t hold the funny sentence, the new friendship, or the reason that moment mattered that day.

Little Moments grew from wanting one simple place where a photo, a child, a caption, and a school year could stay together—and where those memories could keep growing across the years.

Questions?

A little help for Little Moments.

The quick answers to the things teachers are most likely to wonder about this one.

Do the photos stay organized by child?Yes. Each child has their own profile, and every saved moment stays attached to that child.
What happens when I teach the same child another year?You keep the same child profile and start a new school-year section. Their older moments stay right where they are.
Can I print or export the memories?That’s part of the plan. Little Moments is being designed so a child’s saved memories can eventually become a printable year-end portfolio.
Is this for assessment documentation?Not really. Little Moments is for the classroom memories you want to keep. Little Evidence is the Little App we’re building for observations, objectives, and documentation.
