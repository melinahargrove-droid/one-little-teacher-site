Find and name colors

Listen for a named mess, then match its picture. Find that color again at bath time.

02

Count each spot once

Tap Dog’s spots or use Count with me. A numeral and ten-frame show how many there are altogether.

03

Notice one more, one fewer

See the total grow as spots are added and shrink as they wash away. Dog’s black ear spot always stays.

A spotty adventure in three steps

1. Find a mess

Choose Start Dog’s day. Tap the matching object to give Dog one more colorful spot.

2. Stop and count

Tap each spot once, or choose Count with me for a guided count. Use Next mess when everyone is ready.

3. Scrub Dog clean

Rub the named spot, drag the sponge over it, or tap it three times. Count what remains—all the way back to one.

You guide the pace. Teacher settings offer a short 5-spot round or a full 10-spot day, with discoveries in story order or mixed up. Choose Just bath time to begin with washing instead.

A colorful extension to story time

Pair the game with Dog’s Colorful Day by Emma Dodd. After reading, invite children to name colors, count together, and talk about what changes when Dog gains or loses a spot.
