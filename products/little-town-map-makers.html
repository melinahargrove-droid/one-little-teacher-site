Nine places. Your own town. A new route to discover.

Little town. Big thinking.

01

Make and read a map

Arrange nine places on a 3 × 3 town map. Connect each building picture with its place on the map.

02

Talk about position

Use left, right, above, below, and between. Extend to north, east, south, and west when children are ready.

03

Plan a connected route

Look ahead, choose a direction, and follow roads and driveways to a destination. Try another route and explain your choice.

Three ways to explore your town

Build

Choose a building, then select a lot to place it. Move or swap buildings to change your town. Remove, Undo, and Example town help you try a new plan.

Detective

Choose picture, position-word, or compass clues. Listen, look across your town, and select the building that answers the clue. The clues follow your layout.

Deliveries

Find the destination, then use the lit direction buttons or keyboard arrow keys to drive the truck. Follow the connected roads and the final driveway to the door.

Start with a conversation. Ask “What is beside the school?” or “Which way will you go next?” Picture clues offer an accessible starting point; position and compass clues add challenge. The roads stay connected while building positions can change.
