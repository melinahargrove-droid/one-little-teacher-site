How to play

1. Open the workshop

Choose Open the workshop. Look at the animal reference and listen to the clue for the next costume piece.

2. Choose a matching piece

Tap a choice or drag it onto Turkey. A matching piece is added to the disguise; a different choice brings a gentle clue to try again.

3. Look, compare, continue

Choose Next piece, then Next animal. After three pieces, discuss the disguise. Finish with a parade of the costumes you made.

You guide the pace. Teacher’s corner offers 1–5 disguises, each animal appearing once per game. Keep the animal picture visible for matching, or hide it for a listening challenge; names and clues remain visible. Turn recorded voice on or off.

Ways to play

Picture-supported matching

Keep the animal picture visible while children compare the pieces.

Listening challenge

Hide the reference picture and use the spoken and written clues.

A short or full workshop

Choose 1–5 disguises for the next game. Each disguise has three pieces.
