Two ways to help Frankie

Explore

Choose a bandage and place it on a body part. Drag and drop, or tap a bandage and then tap the part. Hear its name and talk about where it is.

Listen & Find

Listen for the requested body part, then place a bandage there. Use Hear again to repeat the direction or Show me for a visual hint. The teacher selects Next turn after a correct placement.

Make room for little details. Switch between Body view and Face close-up. Eyes receive a soft eye patch rather than a bandage. Teacher settings control the number of turns, body-part labels, narration, volume, and sound effects. Changing mode or turns starts a new session.
A gentle classroom conversation

Where does it belong?

Invite children to name the body part before placing a bandage. Compare nearby words, such as cheek and chin, or knee and ankle. Use Explore for naming and Listen & Find for following a spoken direction.

This is pretend play for language practice, not a lesson in treating injuries.
