Two ways to grow the wardrobe

Reveal the next item

Look along the clothesline together and predict the next item. One tap on the magic basket reveals the correct piece. The teacher chooses Next when another child is ready. This mode invites discussion; it does not check a child’s answer.

Choose what comes next

Tap the picture that continues the pattern. AB and AAB offer two choices; ABC offers three. A wrong choice gives a try-again message. A correct choice adds the item and locks that turn until the teacher chooses Next.

You set the pace. Teacher settings offer AB, AAB, ABC, or Mixed. Mixed selects one pattern for the whole session—not a new pattern each turn. Choose Classic, Cozy, or Surprise wardrobes and a positive whole number of turns (starts at 20). The starting pattern does not count toward child turns. Narration, sound effects, and slower animation can be adjusted. Apply & restart starts fresh; Close keeps your place.
A clothesline full of conversation

Say it. Spot it. Keep it going.

Read the repeating group aloud with the class: “Hat, socks; hat, socks.” Invite children to tell how they know which item belongs next. Try Reveal for shared predictions, then Choose for a child’s independent selection.

Classic uses hats, socks, and capes; Cozy uses mittens, boots, and scarves. Surprise varies the wardrobe between sessions. There are no scores or timers, so leave time for explanations and another try.
