What’s included

  • One offline HTML game with Listen & Stomp, Match & Stomp, and Floor-Mat Play.
  • Recorded clues and explanations, plus a clue replay control.
  • Teacher settings for shapes, turn count and shape variety.

The game can print selected shape mats and teacher clues from Teacher tools. No separate printable file is included.

How to use

  1. Unzip the download and open its HTML game in your browser.
  2. Choose the shapes and number of turns in Teacher tools.
  3. Choose a mode and listen, look, or move together.
  4. Use Hear the clue to replay a direction and Next to continue at your group’s pace.
