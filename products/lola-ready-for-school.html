Overview

01 · MATCH

Pack with pictures

Keep the list in view and match each pictured item to the school bag.

02 · REMEMBER

Remember the list

Look together, hide the list and pack from memory. Peek whenever needed, with no time limit.

03 · TELL

Tell Lola’s school day

Sequence three pictures: getting ready, learning and playing at school, and being back together with Mommy.

What’s included

  • Three activities in one HTML game.
  • Teacher controls for list size, number of turns, sounds and gentle movement.
  • Undo, unpack and list-peek controls for practice at each child’s pace.

Recorded directions and feedback are included in the game. No printable materials are included.

How to use

  1. Read your copy of Lola Goes to School together.
  2. Unzip the download, open the HTML in Chrome or Edge, and choose a mode.
  3. Begin with picture matching; use memory play as an optional challenge.
  4. Talk about first, next and last. The packing lists are pretend practice, not school supply requirements.
