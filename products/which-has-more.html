Little Play

Which Has More?

Compare groups and decide which has more or fewer.

Free

▶ Play Free

Ways to use it

  • Use more and fewer to compare quantities.

How to play

  1. Choose a counting limit, question count and marker setting.
  2. Read the comparison prompt.
  3. Compare the groups and tap your choice.

Practical details

Teacher setup offers counting limits of 5, 10 or 20 and optional counting markers.

Explore together

Invite children to explain what they notice and talk through their choices.
