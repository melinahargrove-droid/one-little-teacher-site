Overview

Little harvest

Follow the red and green apple prompts, then combine groups with totals up to five.

02 · ADD

Bigger harvest

Gather larger groups with totals up to ten. Count together or choose the total from three answers.

03 · CREATE

Make our own addition story

Choose up to five red and five green apples. Return individual apples or start over, then find how many altogether.

What’s included

  • One standalone HTML game in a ZIP download.

  • Embedded recorded narration and count-together support.

How to use

  1. Unzip the download and open the HTML file in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Choose Little harvest, Bigger harvest, or Make our own addition story.
  3. Tap or click apples to combine the groups, then select the total.
  4. Use Next basket to continue guided play, or Home to choose another mode. Adjust teacher settings to suit your group.
