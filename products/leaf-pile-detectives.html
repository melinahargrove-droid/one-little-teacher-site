How to use

  1. Unzip the download and open the HTML game in a browser.
  2. Choose a mode and number of turns in Teacher setup.
  3. Listen to the clue and choose the matching friend or leaf. In Peekaboo, choose any pile.
  4. Use Next turn to continue at your group’s pace.
