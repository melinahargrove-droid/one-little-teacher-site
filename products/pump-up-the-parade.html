Three simple steps to a parade

Tap and count

Tap the pump to add one puff at a time. The on-screen counter shows progress toward the target.

Send it soaring

When the balloon is full, choose Send to release it into the parade.

Build a class parade

The teacher chooses Next to invite another turn. At the end, see the balloons your class actually completed.

You set the pace. Choose 1–40 turns and a target of 3–10 puffs. Keep the target fixed, or vary it from 3 to your selected maximum. Children tap the pump and choose Send when the balloon is ready. The teacher chooses Next between turns. Applying settings restarts the game.
Build something together

A little puff becomes a big celebration.

Inflate ten kinds of balloons: acorn, apple, bear, deer, dog, fox, owl, pumpkin, squirrel and turkey. Balloon choices cycle through shuffled sets; longer games can include repeats. The final parade reflects the balloons completed during play.

Use it as a short classroom transition or a shared counting activity. Invite children to count each puff together and notice the balloon growing. Adjust the number of turns to fit your group.
