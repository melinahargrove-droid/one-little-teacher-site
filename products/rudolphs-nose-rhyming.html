How to use

  1. Unzip the download and open the HTML file in a modern browser.
  2. Press Play, listen to the rhyme, and tap the color that finishes it.
  3. Use Next rhyme after the answer, then See Rudolph fly after the final rhyme.
  4. Use Teacher settings for practice choices, narration and hints.
