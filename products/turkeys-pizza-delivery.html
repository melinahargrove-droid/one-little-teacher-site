Overview

Build the order

Listen to the farm friend’s request and add the matching number of toppings.

02 · CHECK

Check your pizza

Use counting support, undo a topping, or start over before checking the order.

03 · DELIVER

Visit the next friend

Deliver the checked pizza and move to the next animal’s order.

What’s included

  • One self-contained HTML counting game with recorded narration.
  • Quick-start instructions.
  • Challenges for 1–5, 1–10, or two toppings with 1–5 of each.

Standalone counting activity. No book required. This is a digital download, not a physical item or printable worksheet.

How to use

  1. Unzip the download and open Turkey_Pizza_Delivery.html in a current desktop browser.
  2. Choose your counting challenge in Teacher settings.
  3. Listen to the order and tap or drag toppings onto the pizza.
  4. Select Check pizza, then Deliver pizza and Next animal.

Gameplay works offline after downloading, without an account or subscription. Use a mouse or an interactive board connected to your computer.
