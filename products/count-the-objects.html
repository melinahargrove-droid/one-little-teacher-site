Count the Objects

Count a group of objects and choose the matching number.

Free

▶ Play Free

Ways to use it

  • Connect quantities with written numbers.

How to play

  1. Choose a counting limit, question count and marker setting.
  2. Count the objects shown.
  3. Tap the matching number.

Practical details

Teacher setup offers counting limits of 5, 10 or 20 and optional counting markers.
