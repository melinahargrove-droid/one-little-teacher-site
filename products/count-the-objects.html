Ways to use it

  • Count a group of everyday objects and pick the matching number.
  • Use counting markers to touch and count each object.

How to play

  1. Choose the highest number and how many questions in Teacher Setup.
  2. Count the objects on the mat.
  3. Tap the number that matches.

Practical details

Teacher Setup sets the highest number to count to (5, 10, or 20), the number of questions, and whether counting markers show. Sound and markers can be toggled during play.
