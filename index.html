About One Little Teacher

Useful first. Pretty second. Complicated never.

One Little Teacher grew out of the little classroom problems that add up: labels that take too long, documentation that interrupts teaching, routines that need to move faster, and materials that should be easier to use.

Everything here is built with a teacher's eye for what actually saves time and what actually works with children in the room.
