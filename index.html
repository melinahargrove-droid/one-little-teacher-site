About One Little Teacher

Useful first.
Pretty second.
Complicated never.

One Little Teacher grew out of the little classroom problems that add up: labels that take too long, documentation that interrupts teaching, routines that need to move faster, and materials that should simply be easier to use.

Everything here is made with a teacher's eye for what actually saves time.
