About One Little Teacher

Teacher-created on purpose.

I'm a classroom teacher, and these tools start with the little problems I actually run into while teaching—the routines that take too many clicks, the resources I wish existed, and the jobs that should be simpler than they are.

One Little Teacher is my way of turning those everyday needs into thoughtful tools for other teachers, too.
