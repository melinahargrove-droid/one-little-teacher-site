Where this Little started
The little story behind Little Moments ♥
this one started with the photos ✦

Classrooms are full of moments worth keeping: a block structure a child worked on forever, a funny sentence, a new friendship, a drawing that suddenly looks different from last month.

“I already take the pictures. Why is keeping them organized the hard part?”

I wanted something faster than sorting through a camera roll later, and more personal than dropping photos into a giant folder with no story attached.

So Little Moments became a simple photo journal: take the picture, add the little story, connect it to the child, and let the app keep the years together.

a real classroom moment → a little story → something worth keeping. ♥
Designed for your phone

Made to live where the moments happen.

Little Moments is being designed phone-first so capturing a photo never feels like a desktop task squeezed onto a tiny screen.

Quick access

one tap away when something happens ♥
  1. Open Little Moments from your phone.
  2. Tap to capture a new moment.
  3. Add the child and caption.
  4. Save it before the next classroom thing happens.

Easy to find later

one child. every year. still there. ✦
  1. Search for a child's name.
  2. Open that child's profile.

  4. Look back without digging through your camera roll.
small enough to use every day. organized enough to matter later. ♥
Questions?

A little help for Little Moments.

The quick answers to the things teachers are most likely to wonder about this one.

Will old school years disappear?No. Each child is planned to have one profile with separate yearly sections so previous years stay accessible.
Can I search for one child?Yes. Search by child name is part of the core design.
Will I be able to print the memories?Yes. A printable year-end portfolio is part of the Little Moments plan.
Is Little Moments available yet?Not yet. It is still being built and refined before it is opened for everyday use.
