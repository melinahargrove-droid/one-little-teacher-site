    Check alignment before a full set
    1. Print the two-page calibration on one sheet, using your chosen duplex edge.
    2. Choose Portrait, 100% / Actual Size, matching paper, no headers or footers, and one page per sheet.
    3. Measure both 100 mm lines. If either is off, fix print scaling first.
    4. Hold against light with the FRONT facing you and its heading upright. Match A to A, B to B, C to C and D to D.
    5. For a consistent shift, adjust the back below and reprint. Positive X moves right and positive Y moves down when viewing the back page upright.

    With the FRONT facing you, if a front cross is right of its back cross, subtract that distance from X for long-edge or add it for short-edge. If a front cross is below its back cross, add that distance to Y for long-edge or subtract it for short-edge. Retest after every adjustment. Skew, rotation and inconsistent feed cannot be corrected with these controls.

    Paper alignment is mathematically paired, but has not been tested on a physical printer. Always test one sheet first. Manual reinsertion depends on your printer; use its documented instructions.
