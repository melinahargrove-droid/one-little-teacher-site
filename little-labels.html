The little story behind Little Labels ♥

It started with a classroom problem. I wanted picture labels that actually matched the things my students were using. But making them meant finding pictures, resizing everything, typing labels, formatting pages… and suddenly a “quick” classroom job wasn’t quick at all.

I kept thinking, Why can’t I just take a picture and have the label made for me?

So that little frustration became Little Labels: a tool built to make one of those ordinary teacher jobs much easier.

a real classroom problem → a little idea → one Little App. ♥

Use it like an app

Put Little Labels right on your phone.

Little Labels works in your browser, but you can add it to your Home Screen so it opens from an icon just like an app.

On iPhone / iPad

  1. Open Little Labels in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add.

On Android

  1. Open Little Labels in Chrome.
  2. Open the browser menu.
  3. Choose Add to Home screen or Install app if shown.
  4. Confirm to add it.
Questions?

A little help for Little Labels.

Do I have to download an app?No. Little Labels can run in your browser and can also be added to your phone’s Home Screen.
Can I use it on a computer?Yes. The goal is for Little Labels to be comfortable on both phones and larger screens.
Is it meant only for preschool?No. It began with preschool classroom needs, but the label-making tools are useful anywhere teachers need quick, clear labels.
Need more help?Visit the Support section on One Little Teacher for guides, FAQs, and contact help as they are added.
